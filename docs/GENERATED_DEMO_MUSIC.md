# Generated demo music

`Neon Steps` is reproducible procedural synthesis from
[scripts/compose_demo_dance.py](../scripts/compose_demo_dance.py): oscillators,
seeded noise, a short chord progression and a programmed rhythm. It uses no
external recording, sample pack, voice or artist imitation. This is local
programmatic composition; it is not a TikTok track or a hosted AI music API.

The script and its generated demo audio may be used, modified and distributed
under this repository's [MIT license](../LICENSE), including embedding in the
demo MP4. This permission does not cover stock footage in the demonstration.
There is no assertion of a registered copyright owner, Content ID clearance,
uniqueness against every existing song, or human listening approval.

138 BPM is the programmed score tempo. The score grid is not a measurement of
perceived audio onsets, nor proof that existing choreography/cuts match it.
Track replacement preserves picture timing and requires a new beat analysis
and review before calling the resulting edit beat synchronized.
